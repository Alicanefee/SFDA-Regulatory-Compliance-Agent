[SFDA-MD-5.1]
Saudi FDA — Medical Devices Interim Regulations, Article 5.1
Application for medical device registration shall include: (a) device description
including intended use, principles of operation, and accessories; (b) risk
classification per SFDA-MD-REQ-1 Annex 1; (c) conformity assessment certificate
from a recognized body; (d) device master file (DMF); (e) labelling in Arabic
and English; (f) IFU (Instructions for Use) in Arabic and English.

---
[SFDA-MD-5.2]
Saudi FDA — Medical Devices Interim Regulations, Article 5.2
All submitted technical documentation must be in English, with Arabic translations
of patient-facing materials (labelling, IFU, patient information leaflets).
Technical specifications may be submitted in English only.

---
[SFDA-MD-5.3]
Saudi FDA — Medical Devices Interim Regulations, Article 5.3
The applicant shall appoint a Saudi-based Authorized Representative (AR) holding
a valid SFDA Medical Devices Establishment License. The AR's appointment letter
must be notarized and apostilled in the country of origin.

---
[SFDA-MD-5.4]
Saudi FDA — Medical Devices Interim Regulations, Article 5.4
Clinical evaluation report (CER) is required for all Class IIa, IIb, and III
devices. The CER must follow MEDDEV 2.7/1 rev.4 methodology and include
equivalence analysis, clinical literature review, and post-market surveillance
data from equivalent devices.

---
[SFDA-MD-5.5]
Saudi FDA — Medical Devices Interim Regulations, Article 5.5
Post-Market Surveillance Plan (PMSP) must be submitted as part of the technical
file. The plan shall define: (a) risk-based reporting intervals; (b) complaint
handling procedure; (c) trend reporting thresholds; (d) periodic safety update
report (PSUR) frequency.

---
[SFDA-MD-5.6]
Saudi FDA — Medical Devices Interim Regulations, Article 5.6
Quality Management System (QMS) certificate (ISO 13485:2016) issued by a
Medical Device Single Audit Programme (MDSAP) recognized auditing organization
must accompany the application. The certificate must be valid for the entire
registration period (5 years) or re-issued upon expiry.
