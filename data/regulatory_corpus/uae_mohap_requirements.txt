[MOHAP-MD-2.1]
UAE MoHAP — Federal Law No. 8 of 2023 (Medical Devices), Article 2.1
Application for medical device registration shall include: (a) device
description and intended purpose; (b) risk classification per MoHAP-REQ-2; (c)
Declaration of Conformity (DoC); (d) technical file; (e) IFU in Arabic and
English; (f) labelling in Arabic and English; (g) clinical evaluation report
for Class IIa/IIb/III; (h) risk management file per ISO 14971:2019.

---
[MOHAP-MD-2.2]
UAE MoHAP — Federal Law No. 8 of 2023 (Medical Devices), Article 2.2
Applicant must appoint a UAE-based Local Authorized Representative (LAR) holding
a valid MoHAP Medical Devices License. The LAR's appointment letter must be
legalized by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA).

---
[MOHAP-MD-2.3]
UAE MoHAP — Federal Law No. 8 of 2023 (Medical Devices), Article 2.3
All patient-facing documentation (IFU, labelling, patient leaflets) must be
provided in both Arabic and English. The Arabic version must be reviewed by a
certified Arabic medical translator and stamped by the LAR.

---
[MOHAP-MD-2.4]
UAE MoHAP — Federal Law No. 8 of 2023 (Medical Devices), Article 2.4
Cybersecurity documentation is required for connected medical devices: (a)
cybersecurity risk assessment per IEC 81001-5-1; (b) software bill of materials
(SBOM); (c) threat model; (d) post-market cybersecurity plan (PMCP).

---
[MOHAP-MD-2.5]
UAE MoHAP — Federal Law No. 8 of 2023 (Medical Devices), Article 2.5
Post-Market Surveillance System (PMSS) documentation must be submitted. Must
include: vigilance reporting procedure (FSCA within 10 days of awareness),
trend reporting (quarterly), periodic safety update reports (annually for
Class IIb/III, every 2 years for Class IIa).

---
[MOHAP-MD-2.6]
UAE MoHAP — Federal Law No. 8 of 2023 (Medical Devices), Article 2.6
Data protection compliance statement required for devices processing personal
health data. Must reference UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL) and
provide: (a) data flow diagram; (b) lawful basis for processing; (c) data
retention policy; (d) patient consent mechanism.
